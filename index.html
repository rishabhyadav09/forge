If you're starting out

Start lighter than your ego wants. Week 1 should feel almost too easy. Pick a weight you could do three more reps with. You'll add load for eleven straight weeks — start heavy and you stall by week four.

RPE means reps in reserve. RPE 7 = three more were available. RPE 8 = two. That's how you autoregulate on a bad day without derailing the block.

Eat and sleep like it matters. At 72 kg, roughly 115–145 g of protein a day and 7–8 hours of sleep. Training is the stimulus; food and sleep are where adaptation happens.

Soreness is not the scoreboard. Progress is the bar going up and the intervals getting easier.

Why there's no automatic Garmin sync

Garmin has no personal API. The Connect Developer Program only admits legal entities and rejects personal-use applications; as of 2026 new sign-ups are on hold entirely.

The workaround that works: Garmin Connect auto-syncs activities to Strava, and Strava gives individuals a free personal API key in two minutes.

The unofficial python-garminconnect scrapes Connect directly, but needs your raw credentials and Garmin's March-2026 auth change broke the library underneath it.

Soundtrack
Matched to the day. Aggression on the heavy days, steady BPM on the conditioning days.

For lifting, music works as an arousal switch — tempo matters less than aggression. For conditioning it's the reverse: 150–170 BPM pulls your cadence along with it, which is what Wednesday and Saturday are tuned to.
